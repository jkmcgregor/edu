Instructional Approach

How I design learning for adult students.

Start from the learner

Adult learners arrive with jobs, constraints, and varied backgrounds. I adapt instruction methods to their needs and simplify technical subject matter for foundational IT students.

Make it hands-on

I design assessments and lab assignments that reinforce foundational concepts, so students practice the skill rather than only recognize the term.

Teach from real systems

Examples come from enterprise ERP, servers and monitoring, SQL, APIs, and field service work, which gives abstract topics a concrete context.

Measure what works

I treat training as something to measure. A program I built for new hires raised knowledge retention by 50%.
